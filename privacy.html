Privacy and Google data use

Updated October 20, 2026.

Mint is operated by its owner for personal financial recordkeeping. Google Drive data is used to read requests and supporting evidence in configured folders and to publish matching results, budget views, activity, source status and reviews.

The application requests Drive read-only access and access to files created or explicitly granted to the application. Google’s read-only permission can cover the owner's Drive broadly; Mint additionally restricts processing to its configured folders. The owner controls which conversational assistants are connected to the private Drive.

The authoritative database, source evidence and local backups are stored on owned computers. Google hosts the private Drive transport and views. Financial information intentionally supplied to a connected assistant is also processed by that assistant's provider under its own policies. OAuth credentials are kept separately on owned infrastructure and are not placed in Drive requests, public source code or conversational messages.

Mint does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. Mint’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. This describes Mint's own handling; third-party services have their own published policies.

Records and backups are retained under the owner's control. The owner can delete local records and Drive files and revoke the application's access in Google Account permissions. Revoking access stops future authorized retrieval; it does not delete existing copies or backups.

This is a personal-use application with no public account service. The owner is the operator and support contact shown on the Google consent screen.
