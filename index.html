Mint Personal Finance

Mint is a private, personally operated financial record system. It records the owner's reported expenses, preserves context, and calculates budget views on the owner's Mac Mini.

The Google Drive integration transports requests and results and publishes derived views in the owner's private Drive. Authorized conversational assistants use those views and requests as the owner's financial interface.

This application is for the owner's personal use. It does not offer public sign-up or perform payments, transfers, trades or borrowing.
