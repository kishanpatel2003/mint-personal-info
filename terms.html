Personal-use operating terms

Updated October 20, 2026.

Mint is personally operated software for its owner's financial records. There is no public registration, subscription or financial-service offering.

The owner's Mini ledger is authoritative. Drive views are derived snapshots. A submitted request is only a saved financial fact after its matching committed result is available. Unknown balances, missing history and stale sources must remain explicit.

Connected assistants act within the owner's specific instructions. Logging expenses or answering budget questions does not authorize payments, transfers, trades, borrowing, paid services or contacting other people.

The operator controls access and can stop the service or revoke Google authorization. Google and connected services apply their own terms to their respective services.
